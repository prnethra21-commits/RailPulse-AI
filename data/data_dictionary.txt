RailPulse AI - Day 1 Data Dictionary

train_id
Unique identifier for the train.

route_id
Identifier of the railway route.

station_id
Identifier of the station where the train record is recorded.

timestamp
Date and time associated with the train's station record.

scheduled_arrival
Scheduled arrival time at the station.

actual_arrival
Actual arrival time at the station.

scheduled_departure
Scheduled departure time from the station.

actual_departure
Actual departure time from the station.

speed
Train speed at the recorded station/section, in km/h.

distance
Distance travelled along the route from the starting station, in km.

current_delay
Current train delay compared with the scheduled time, in minutes.

actual_section_time
Actual travel time taken for the section leading to the current station, in minutes.
This is the target variable that can be used for section travel-time prediction.
